Welcome to

THE WORLD OF GARFIELD

Garfield is a lazy, lasagna-loving orange cat created by Jim Davis. He loves food, naps, and making fun of Odie. He hates Mondays, exercise, and kittens. This is his world — where eating and sleeping are the only things that matter!

"I’m not overweight. I’m just too short for my weight." — Garfield

🐱 Meet the Characters

Garfield

The star of the show! Orange, fluffy, loves lasagna and naps, hates Mondays. He is smart, sarcastic, and adorable!

Odie

Garfield's best friend (and favorite punching bag). A happy, drooling, lovable dog who doesn't say much but is always there!

Jon Arbuckle

The owner of Garfield and Odie. A kind-hearted cartoonist who tries his best to keep Garfield happy and well-fed!

👋 About Us

Welcome to Garfield Paradise — your cozy home for everything about Garfield!

We are dedicated fans who love the world's most famous lasagna-loving cat. Our goal is to bring together Garfield fans from everywhere to celebrate the humor, the comics, and the joy that Jim Davis created.

Whether you've been following Garfield since the very first strip or you're just getting to know him now — you're welcome here! 🧡

"Love me, feed me, never leave me." — Garfield
